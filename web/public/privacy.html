Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

1. What we collect

We collect account identifiers such as email address and authentication records. When you import a file, we process the execution fields needed to reconstruct trades, including symbol, side, quantity, price, timestamp, account reference, fees, and import metadata. We collect profile details only when you provide them.

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, import and deduplicate records, reconstruct trades, run deterministic historical analyses, secure the service, and respond to support requests. We do not use your records to make decisions for you or to train an advertising profile.

3. Visibility

Your account and trade history are private by default. If you enable public profile visibility, the product may display the profile fields and aggregate metrics described in settings. Raw executions, account numbers, and uploaded files are not intended to be public.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information to service providers operating the product, when you direct us to, or when required to comply with law, protect rights, or prevent abuse. We do not redistribute raw market data.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain account and imported records while your account is active or as needed for the purposes described here. You may request deletion through the product. Backups and legally required records may persist for a limited period.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet service is guaranteed secure. Use a unique password and notify silas@rulemirror.com if you suspect unauthorized access.

7. Your choices

You can update profile details, turn public visibility off, sign out, and delete your account. Browser-local preferences may require clearing this browser separately.

8. Children and international use

RuleMirror is not directed to children who cannot lawfully agree to these Terms. Privacy protections may differ by location; contact silas@rulemirror.com with questions about applicable rights.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above indicates the latest revision. For privacy questions or requests, use silas@rulemirror.com.
